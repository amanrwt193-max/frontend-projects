Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy outlines the terms under which refunds and cancellations are handled for services provided by iTech Infosysteam.

1. General Principle

Because most of our services are custom, digital and labour-intensive (account management, advertising, design, development), refunds are limited and assessed on a case-by-case basis.

2. Cancellation by Client

  • Before work begins: If you cancel after paying an advance but before any work has started, we will refund the amount after deducting a small administrative fee (up to 10%).
  • After work has started: Fees for work already completed or resources already allocated are non-refundable. Any unused portion of a retainer may be adjusted or refunded at our discretion.
  • Written cancellation request must be sent to care@infotech.in.

3. Cancellation by iTech Infosysteam

We reserve the right to cancel or pause services in case of non-payment, violation of marketplace policies, or misuse of our services. In such cases, fees for work already performed remain payable.

4. Refund Eligibility

  • Refunds are generally not available for completed deliverables (websites, creatives, completed campaigns, photography, etc.).
  • If we fail to deliver a clearly defined milestone due to our own fault, we will either complete the work at no extra cost or offer a partial refund proportional to the undelivered portion.
  • Marketplace advertising spend (Amazon Ads, Flipkart Ads, Google Ads, etc.) is paid directly to the platforms and is non-refundable through us.

5. Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within 7–14 business days to the original payment method. Bank processing times may vary.

6. How to Request a Refund or Cancellation

Email us at care@infotech.in with:

  • Your name and registered contact details
  • Invoice / project reference number
  • Reason for the request

Our team will review and respond within 3–5 business days.
